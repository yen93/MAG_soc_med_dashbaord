Route notes

In priority order — highest-leverage move first.
  1. Highest leverageRebuild the LinkedIn camp deliberately: post 2–3×/week, have James engage from his personal profile, and ask five past corporate clients to follow and comment. It's the lowest score sitting in front of the highest-value audience.
  2. Quick fixRepoint the website's LinkedIn icon from James's personal profile to the Company Page — every visitor who clicks it today is a follower the Page score above never sees.
  3. Low effortGive Facebook a standing cross-post habit from Instagram. It won't close the gap alone, but it stops the page from going quiet between keynotes.
  4. CompoundingTurn stage reach into feed reach: a single call-to-action at the end of each keynote pointing to Instagram could move the needle on ~1.8K followers faster than any content tweak.

How this was scored

Every score above is built only from what's visible to a logged-out visitor as of 7 August 2026: bio text, story highlights, follower/following counts, visible posting cadence, page metadata and website structure. Nothing here uses a connected API or the account owner's private dashboards.

That means it deliberately leaves out reach, impressions, saves, click-through and audience demographics — the numbers that live inside Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn Page Analytics and the site's own analytics tool. Those are the real next step: plug them in and every score here gets sharper.

Follower counts fluctuate and different sources (a profile page vs. a search index) can disagree by small margins — treat the numbers above as directional (“~1.8K”, not a live counter), not a live feed.
