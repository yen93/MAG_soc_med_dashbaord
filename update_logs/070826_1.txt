DELIVERABLE PRODUCED THIS SESSION
-----------------------------------
A single self-contained HTML page, "MAG Social & Web Health Report", designed
from scratch (not a copy of the reference tool's exact layout, since its
finished design was never observed — see Research above) and published as a
Claude Artifact (hosted on claude.ai, NOT committed into this repository).

Design approach:
  - Visual identity: "field audit / expedition dashboard", tying into James
    Castrission's explorer background (compass mark, route-note numbered
    recommendations, dashed "trail" section rule).
  - Typography: Big Shoulders Display (headings/scores) + Work Sans (body),
    both downloaded from Google Fonts and inlined as base64 @font-face data
    URIs so the artifact renders correctly under the platform's content
    security policy (no external font requests). Injected via a short Python
    substitution script rather than pasting the ~115KB of base64 through the
    chat, to avoid bloating the conversation.
  - Score gauges: pure CSS conic-gradient rings (270° arc, dial pattern) —
    no JS or hand-authored SVG paths needed.
  - Palette: dark "glacier navy" theme by default with a light theme
    override (both themes token-driven via CSS custom properties), ice-blue
    accent kept separate from semantic status colors (green/amber/red).

Scores shown on the published artifact (hand-derived from the public data
above, NOT run through this repo's js/scoring.js engine or data/metrics.json
— see as_built.txt section 6 for why these two are separate):
  Website    84 / 100  (Solid)
  Instagram  79 / 100  (Solid)
  Facebook   61 / 100  (Steady)
  LinkedIn   46 / 100  (At risk)
  Composite  68 / 100  (Steady)

Each channel card includes 4 category sub-scores, a "takeaway" line, and the
page ends with a prioritized "Route notes" recommendation list (fix the
website's LinkedIn icon — it links to James's personal profile instead of
the Company Page; rebuild the LinkedIn presence; add a Facebook cross-post
habit; convert stage reach into Instagram follower growth) plus a
methodology section disclosing that all scores are estimated from public,
logged-out signals captured 2026-08-07 — not private platform analytics.
